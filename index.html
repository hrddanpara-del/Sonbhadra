Shri Renukeshwar Mahadev Mandir

Renukeshwar Mahadev Temple is situated in Renukoot in Sonbhadra district, Uttar Pradesh. It is located adjoining the Rihand River which is also called Renu River, from where this temple got its name. The temple is dedicated to Mahadev or Lord Shiva. The temple was built by Birla Group in the year 1972.

Vaishno Devi temple | Dala

The Maa Vaishno Devi Shaktipeeth Dham Mandir in Dala, Sonbhadra is a prominent regional pilgrimage site designed as a lifelike replica of the famous Vaishno Devi temple in Katra, Jammu.

Jwala Devi Temple

Jwaladevi Temple (ज्वालादेवी मन्दिर) is located in Shaktinagar which is in Sonbhadra district of Uttar Pradesh. The Jwaladevi temple is dedicated to Goddess Jwala Ji . This is an age old Ashtagrih temple of Jwala Devi & one of the 51 Shakta pithas of India. The old temple is believed to be 1000 years old.

Rihand Dam, Pipari, Govind Ballabh Pant Sagar

The Rihand Dam, also known as the Govind Ballabh Pant Sagar Dam, is a concrete gravity dam located at Pipri in the Sonbhadra district of Uttar Pradesh. Built on the Rihand River (a tributary of the Son River), it features India's largest artificial lake/reservoir by volume.

Lakhaniya Dari Waterfall

Lakhaniya Dari Waterfall is a picturesque, plunge-style waterfall located in the Mirzapur district of Uttar Pradesh, near Ahraura. Cascading from a height of approximately 150 meters into a rocky pool, it is a popular day trip and scenic picnic spot for nature lovers and adventure enthusiasts.
